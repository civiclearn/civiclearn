# How Our PD3 Simulation Works

## A training program — not a replica

Our PD3 simulations are designed to **teach you how to prepare**, not to replicate exam conditions exactly. We've added helpful elements that the real exam doesn't have — on purpose. Think of them as training wheels: they show you what good preparation looks like, so that when exam day comes, you'll know exactly how to attack each task on your own.

Here's an honest look at what's different — and why.

---

## What's the same as the real PD3

- **The format is real.** Every section of our simulation matches the official PD3 structure: Læseforståelse 1, 2A, 2B, 3, Skriftlig Del 1 and Del 2, and Mundtlig Del 1 and Del 2. Same number of questions, same task types, same time limits.
- **The difficulty is real.** Our texts and topics are at B2 level, just like the actual exam. Nothing is simplified.
- **The grading scale is real.** We use the official 7-trins-skalaen (-3 to 12) and the same pass criteria: a weighted average of at least 02, with mundtlig counting double.
- **You still have to perform.** In the writing sections, you write every word yourself. In the oral sections, you speak for 2 minutes with no script, no subtitles, and no second chances — just like the real thing. Nobody is helping you there.

---

## What's different — and why

### 📖 Læseforståelse (Reading)

| Real exam | Our simulation | Why |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Printed text booklet + exercise booklet | Digital texts on screen | Same content, different medium. You learn to find answers quickly regardless of format. |
| No aids allowed | No aids (same) | We respect this constraint. |

**Bottom line:** Our reading sections are very close to the real thing.

### ✍️ Skriftlig fremstilling (Writing)

| Real exam | Our simulation | Why |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Handwritten or typed on exam computer | Typed in browser | Identical task — you still write everything yourself. |
| You get the assignment on exam day | You get the assignment on screen | Same. |
| Graded by human examiners (weeks later) | Graded by AI (within minutes) | Instant feedback helps you learn faster. Our AI evaluator is calibrated to grade fairly at B2 level. |
| No feedback on what to improve | Detailed feedback with strengths, weaknesses, and specific mistakes | This is the big advantage of practicing with us. On the real exam, you just get a number. Here, you learn *why*. |

**Bottom line:** The writing task itself is identical. The feedback is a bonus that only a training program can give you.

### 🎙️ Mundtlig Del 1 (Oral — Presentation + Interview)

This is where we're most intentionally different.

| Real exam | Our simulation | Why |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| You receive **only a topic and a focus angle** (e.g., "Overvægt og usund kost — Hvorfor spiser vi dårligere end vi ved vi burde?") | You receive the topic **plus** key facts, useful vocabulary, and a suggested structure | These extras teach you *how to prepare*. After 2–3 simulations, you'll know instinctively: find statistics, learn key terms, build a 5-part structure. |
| 5 working days to prepare | Prepare at your own pace | Practice without pressure first. |
| Face-to-face with examiner + censor | Record yourself; AI evaluates | You can practice as many times as you want. |
| Examiner asks follow-up questions live | AI asks follow-up questions | Similar dynamic, different medium. |

**The key point:** On the real exam, you get a bare topic and must figure out the rest yourself. Our scaffolding — the key facts, vocabulary, and structure suggestions — is there to show you what "well-prepared" looks like. By the time you've done a few simulations, you'll build that scaffolding yourself automatically.

And remember: **you still have to talk for 2 minutes.** No amount of key facts on screen will do that for you. That's all you.

### 🎙️ Mundtlig Del 2 (Oral — Conversation)

| Real exam | Our simulation | Why |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Examiner shows you pictures, describes a situation, asks questions | AI describes a situation, asks questions | Very similar. |
| Topic is completely unknown | Topic is completely unknown (same) | No scaffolding here — just like the real exam. |

**Bottom line:** Del 2 is close to real conditions. You walk in cold and talk.

## How to get the most out of our simulations

1. **First 1–2 simulations: Use everything.** Read the key facts, study the vocabulary, follow the suggested structure. Understand what a well-prepared presentation looks like.

2. **Next simulations: Wean yourself off.** Try preparing your presentation *before* opening the key facts. Then check: did you cover the important points? Did you find good vocabulary on your own?

3. **Final preparation: Ignore the scaffolding.** Read only the topic and focus angle. Prepare as if it were the real exam. Use the key facts only *after* your attempt to see what you missed.

4. **Always do the oral recording.** The writing sections are important, but mundtlig counts double. Every recording you make — even a bad one — builds confidence and fluency.

---

## One more thing

Our AI evaluator is designed to be encouraging and fair. It's calibrated for B2 level, which means it won't punish you for informal language in an informal email, and it won't nitpick stylistic choices that a real examiner wouldn't care about.

That said, it's a machine — not a human examiner. Use the scores as a guide, not a guarantee. If you're consistently scoring 7 or above across our simulations, you're in very good shape for the real thing.
