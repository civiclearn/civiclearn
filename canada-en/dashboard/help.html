Frequently asked questions
How can I completely reset my training?
Click the button below to start from scratch. This will erase all your statistics and progress, while keeping your personal settings.

How do I enable daily reminders?
Simply turn on the switch below to receive a notification each evening when your Daily Test is ready. You can enable or disable it at any time according to your preferences.

Are the test questions official?
No. The questions on CivicLearn are educational simulations written from the content of the official study guide, Discover Canada. They reflect the spirit, difficulty and format of the real citizenship test, but they are not taken from any government source.

Official test questions are not available to anyone: Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) does not publish them.
What is the difference between the test modes?
CivicLearn offers several training modes adapted to different needs:
  • Test Simulation: Mirrors the official test format (20 questions, timer, results at the end; pass mark 15/20, or 75%).
  • Quick Test: 5 random questions, no time limit, instant correction.
  • Custom Test (by topic): You choose one or more topics. This mode is progressive: questions repeat until all are answered correctly.
  • Frequent Traps: Targeted practice focusing on the most common mistakes, without a timer.
How does the progressive mode work?
Progressive mode does not stop after a fixed number of questions. It automatically repeats incorrectly answered questions until all are mastered. When none remain, a message appears: “All mastered 🎉”.
Why do some tests show “Restart” or “Review mistakes” buttons?
These buttons appear only in non-progressive tests (such as the Simulation). The test ends once all questions are answered, and you can then review your mistakes or start a new attempt.
Which mode should I use to prepare effectively?
For a complete preparation:
  • Start with the Custom Test to build solid knowledge topic by topic.
  • Use the Quick Test for light daily revision.
  • Finish with a Test Simulation to check your level under real conditions.
Where can I read the study guide?
The official study guide, Discover Canada, can be downloaded as a PDF from the dashboard. The chapters are also available in the guide section of the side menu, each with a CivicLearn summary and an audio recording.
In “Reading Assistance” mode, why does the voice sound foreign?

In “Reading Assistance” mode, why does the voice sound foreign?

CivicLearn uses your browser’s built-in text-to-speech system to read text aloud. Normally, English text is read with an English voice. However, if your device or browser does not have a suitable English voice installed, it may fall back to another default voice and read the English with a foreign accent.

How do I fix it?

  1. Install an English voice in your device settings:
    • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English
    • Android: Settings → System → Text-to-speech output → Voice → English
    • Computer: Check your system’s text-to-speech / read-aloud settings.
  2. Then reload the CivicLearn page.
How do I listen to the chapters in audio?
Open the guide section in the side menu, then select the chapter you want and press ▶️ Listen. You can pause or resume playback at any time.
What is the “Frequent Traps” section?
This section groups the questions that users most often answer incorrectly. It helps you focus on weak points and practise more efficiently before attempting a full simulation.
