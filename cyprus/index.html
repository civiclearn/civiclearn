The exam

What is the Cyprus citizenship test?

A written examination that applicants for Cypriot citizenship by naturalisation must pass, covering the history, culture and institutions of the Republic of Cyprus.

Format
25 multiple-choice questions

The test takes 45 minutes and is sat in Greek. Questions are drawn from 14 official topic areas.

Pass mark
50% or 60%

The threshold depends on your application route: 50% for some routes, 60% for citizenship. Our simulation uses the 60% threshold (15/25).

Topics
14 official topic areas

Political system and institutions, geography, modern history since 1960, customs and traditions, current affairs, economy, public holidays, telecommunications, climate, transport, entry requirements, languages and religions, health and safety, currency.
