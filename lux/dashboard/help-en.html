Frequently asked questions

How do daily reminders work?›
Daily reminders are short notifications that help you keep a regular study rhythm. When enabled, you receive a notification when your daily test is ready.

Are the test questions official?›
No. The questions on CivicLearn are educational simulations designed to reflect the structure, difficulty, and key topics of the Luxembourg Vivre-Ensemble test.

They are not official questions and are not taken from any government database. Official questions are not publicly released by Luxembourg authorities.
What do the questions shown on the dashboard represent?›
The questions shown on the dashboard are part of a sequential practice mode that includes all questions from the Luxembourg question bank, presented one by one in a fixed order.

You can move to the next or previous question, allowing you to skip questions and return to them later. This is the only practice mode where questions can be skipped.

Your progress is automatically saved: if you leave the page or refresh it, the dashboard will reopen on the last question you viewed.
What does the “Estimated score” in the sidebar mean?›
It is an estimate of the score you would get in a simulation today, out of 40, based on how many questions you have mastered in each topic, weighted like the official test (10 history, 20 institutions, 10 rights). The small mark on the bar is the pass threshold of 28. It updates every time you answer a question anywhere on the platform.
What is the purpose of the “My List” feature?›
Each question includes a small star icon that allows you to add it to your personal review list. By clicking this star, the question is saved to “My List”.

This feature lets you keep track of important or difficult questions so you can review them later and focus your revision more efficiently.
What is the difference between the test modes?›
CivicLearn offers several training modes adapted to different needs:
  • Test Simulation: Mirrors the official test format (multiple questions, timer, results at the end).
  • Quick Test: 5 random questions, no time limit, instant correction.
  • Test by Topic: You choose one or more topics. This mode is adaptive: questions repeat until all are answered correctly.
  • Common Traps: Targeted practice focusing on the most frequent mistakes, without a timer.
How does the progressive mode work?›
Progressive mode does not stop after a fixed number of questions. Incorrectly answered questions are automatically repeated until all are mastered. When none remain, a message appears: “All mastered”.
Why do some tests show “Restart” or “Review mistakes” buttons?›
These buttons appear only in non-progressive tests (such as the Simulation). The test ends once all questions are answered, and you can then review mistakes or start a new attempt.
Which mode should I use to prepare effectively?›
For a complete preparation:
  • Start with the Test by Topic to build solid knowledge by topic.
  • Use the Quick Test for light daily revision.
  • Finish with a Test Simulation to assess your level under realistic conditions.
In “Reading Assistance” mode, why does the voice sound foreign?›
CivicLearn uses your browser’s built-in text-to-speech system to read questions aloud.

If your device does not have a high-quality voice installed for the selected language (English, French, German, or Luxembourgish), the browser may fall back to a generic system voice or a different accent.

To improve pronunciation, install additional voices in your device’s accessibility or speech settings, then reload the page.
How is the Vivre-Ensemble test simulation scored?›
The CivicLearn simulation faithfully reproduces the official format of the Luxembourg Vivre-Ensemble test.

Each simulation contains 40 questions, distributed exactly as in the official test:
  • 10 questions on the history of Luxembourg
  • 10 questions on fundamental rights
  • 20 questions on institutions
To pass, you must answer at least 28 questions correctly out of 40.

You have one hour to complete the test. This time limit is applied to replicate real exam conditions.

All questions have the same weight: there is no penalty for incorrect answers and no requirement to pass any specific topic at 100%.
What is the “Common Traps” section?›
This section groups questions that users most often answer incorrectly. It helps you focus on weak points and prepare more efficiently before attempting a full simulation.
Can I use CivicLearn on my phone?›

Can I study offline?›
Yes. Once pages are loaded, most features (tests, flashcards, reference texts) remain available without an internet connection, except for submitting the contact form.
