Background

What is Finland's new citizenship test?

The Finnish government is introducing a civic knowledge requirement for citizenship applicants, with legislation expected to take effect in January 2027.

The test
Civic knowledge about Finland

The test will assess fundamental knowledge needed to live and participate in Finnish society — constitutional values, democracy, and the rule of law.

Legislation
Bill in Parliament spring 2026

The draft legislation was open for public comment in early 2026. Parliamentary hearings are expected in April 2026, with entry into force targeted for January 2027.

Topics
5 topic areas

Constitutional values and democracy, rights and obligations, Finnish history and culture, society and everyday life, equality and safety.

Format
Computer-based, multiple-choice

The test will be administered digitally in Finnish or Swedish. A university is preparing the official test material on behalf of Migri.

Administered by
Migri

The Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) will oversee the test. Exemptions apply for those with a Finnish higher education degree or who have passed the matriculation exam.

Age range
18–64 years

The civic knowledge requirement applies to citizenship applicants aged 18 to 64. Study materials will be publicly available.
