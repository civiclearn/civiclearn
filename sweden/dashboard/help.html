Frequently Asked Questions

What does this training cover? ›
This platform prepares you for the upcoming Swedish citizenship civics test (medborgarskapsprov). Questions cover democracy, laws, rights, Swedish society, history, and geography — based on topics announced by the Swedish government.
Is this the official test? ›
No. The official test has not yet been published. Our questions are based on the government's announced topic areas and publicly available civic knowledge material. When the official test format is released, we will update accordingly.
How does the wave system work in topic tests? ›
When you practise by topic, questions you answer incorrectly come back in the next wave. This continues until you answer every question correctly. This ensures genuine mastery, not just memorisation.
What is the simulation mode? ›
The simulation presents 25 questions in 45 minutes with an 80% pass threshold (20/25), mirroring the expected format of the real test. Use it as a rehearsal after practising by topic.
Can I use this on my phone? ›
Yes, the platform is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. You can add it to your home screen for app-like access.
