# Medborgerskabsprøven – læremateriale august 2026 vs august 2025

How the diff was made: the old site JSON (`medborgerskab/banks/laerermateriale.json`, built from the Aug 2025 PDF) was flattened to sentences and compared chapter by chapter against a fresh text extraction of the Aug 2026 PDF (photo captions, running headers and page numbers stripped). Same 26 fakta-ark, same glossary (244 terms, only a typo fix). Everything below is a real content change; wording-only tidy-ups are left out.

## What changed in the material (by fakta-ark)

**1 Skole** – fransk added to the list of folkeskole subjects. Specialundervisning now "mindst ni timer" (was "mere end ni"). New sentence: the school can in some cases give standpunktskarakterer in 6. and 7. klasse. "Elevplanen" renamed "Meddelelsesbogen" in the skole-hjem-samtale list.

**2 Uddannelse** – professionsbachelor examples now: folkeskolelærer, jordemoder, sygeplejerske, socialrådgiver, pædagog, fysioterapeut, diplomingeniør (the sentence "de fleste arbejder i det offentlige" is gone). University fields: "humanistisk videnskab" (was humaniora), "kunst (fx design)" removed, "civilingeniør" (was ingeniørvidenskab). Master/diplom: "to til tre år" (was tre år).

**4 Arbejdsmarked** – overenskomst period "to eller tre år" (was to år). Employment figure rewritten: "Cirka 73 procent af borgerne mellem 16 og 66 år har et lønmodtagerarbejde" (was "lidt mere end tre ud af fire mellem 15 og 64"). Fagforening: "to ud af tre (68 procent)". A-kasser are "medlemsforeninger" (was "private foreninger"). Dagpenge now require having worked before unemployment. A-kasse holds "samtaler" (not møder) and fills in "Min Plan" itself. After **four** months (was three) **kommunen** (was jobcenteret) takes over. "Uddannelseshjælp" dropped from the kontanthjælp sentence.

**5 Erhvervsliv** – trade partners 2025: USA 14,1 %, Tyskland 12,7 %, Sverige 7,8 %, Holland 5,4 %, Storbritannien 5,2 %, Kina 4,8 %, Norge 4,0 % (order unchanged).

**6 Velfærd** – folkepension 67 år "i 2026". Erhvervsakademi added to the free higher-education examples. Libraries also lend lydbøger and e-bøger.

**8 Forenings- og fritidsliv** – new paragraph: part of foreningslivet is now digital (onlinefællesskaber, sociale medier).

**9 Sundhed** – the sentence about 16–17-year-olds losing the right to buy alcohol over 6 % "fra 2025" is removed (the 18-year rule is simply stated).

**10 Ligestilling** – promotion example now "seksuelle orientering, handicap eller alder" (was køn). Women in Folketinget: "86 ud af 179 medlemmer (48 procent)" (was 44). New caption-level sentence: the rainbow flag is a symbol for LGBT+ people.

**13 Folketing og regering** – the 2022 majority government (S, V, M) is gone. Now: "Det gælder også den nuværende regering, der består af fire partier Socialdemokratiet, SF, Moderaterne og Radikale Venstre" – i.e. a **minority** government. Election was 3 June 2026 (photo caption).

**14 Lokale selvstyre** – regionsråd have "mellem 25 og 47 medlemmer" (was 41 each). "De fem regioner" → "Regionerne" (the 2027 merger sentence is still there).

**15 Valg og partier** – turnout "cirka 85 procent" (was 80–90). Kommunal/regionsvalg 2025: 69 % (was 2021: 67 %). Women after the 2026 election: 49 % (note: fakta-ark 10 says 48 % – the source is inconsistent). Parties in Folketinget after 2026, in order of votes: Socialdemokratiet, SF, Venstre, Liberal Alliance, Dansk Folkeparti, Moderaterne, Det Konservative Folkeparti, Enhedslisten, Radikale Venstre, Danmarksdemokraterne, Alternativet, Borgernes Parti (Nye Borgerlige out, Borgernes Parti in). Greenland: Inuit Ataqatigiit and Nalaraq (Siumut out). Party members: 106.000 (was 110.000).

**16 Rettigheder og pligter** – værnepligt "11 måneder i Forsvaret" (was fire, with the 2026 extension announced). The 1 July 2025 women's conscription sentence kept.

**17 Religion** – folkekirke 70 % in 2026 (was 71). Membership "ved at blive døbt". Funeral rules rewritten: a funeral can be kirkelig (folkekirke priest or another trossamfund's priest) or borgerlig, and it is the deceased's or the bereaved's wishes that decide – the old rule "kirkelig begravelse only for members" is gone. Recognised trossamfund: 435 (was 436), requirements now also mention ritualer and trosgrundlag. Sentence "Mange af verdens religioner findes i Danmark" removed.

**18 Danmark og omverdenen** – 21 of 27 EU countries use the euro (was 19). EU presidency: "Sidste danske EU-formandskab var i 2025" (the 2012 and "1 July–31 Dec 2025" sentences gone). Folketing turnout 2026: 84 %. Udviklingsbistand 2026: ca. 23 mia. kr. (was 22).

**19 Geografi og befolkning** – indvandrere/efterkommere "cirka en million (17 procent)" (was 16). Aarhus ca. 300.000 (was 280.000), Odense ca. 200.000 (180.000), Aalborg ca. 125.000 (120.000), Esbjerg ca. 75.000 (72.000).

**21 Historie efter 1945** – life expectancy 2024–25: women 83,9 / men 80,3 (was 83,7 / 79,9); fertility 1,5 "i 2025".

**24 Forsvar og sikkerhed** – new: President Donald Trump has expressed the wish that Greenland become part of the USA; rejected by both the Greenlandic and Danish governments.

**25 Diskrimination** – hate-crime statistics now 2022–24: 2.108 cases; 1.022 (48 %) race/ethnicity, 756 (36 %) religion, 435 (21 %) sexual orientation; 702 per year ≈ two a day; 430 against Jews, i.e. every fifth hate crime (was every tenth). The 2017 court case is replaced by a 2025 case (three people sentenced to 6–10 months for assaulting two homosexual men, one also for filming and sharing it).

**26 Klima** – emissions 1990→2024 down ca. 53 % (was 2023, 51 %). Renewable energy 50 % "i 2025". Wind produced 52 % of electricity in 2025 (was "over halvdelen" in 2023).

Unchanged: fakta-ark 3, 7, 11, 12, 20, 22, 23 (typo fixes only) and the glossary.
