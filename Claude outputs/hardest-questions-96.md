# The Hardest Citizenship Test Questions in the World: Round 1 shortlist

28 Sep 2026 · for Denis to strike and approve before the page gets built

## How to use this

- There are **129 candidates across 16 countries**. The page needs about 40, so strike freely.
- Mark each one **KEEP** or **STRIKE**, or just send me the IDs you want to strike. Unmarked ones count as "keep if we need them".
- **📊 Data** means the question is ranked by the real share of CivicLearn users who answered it wrong. The user count is shown, and anything under 100 users is marked *indicative*.
- **✋ Hand-picked** means the bank has too few users for a ranking (Cyprus, UK, Australia, Finland, Lithuania, Portugal, Slovakia), so I chose obscure dates, trick wording and counter-intuitive facts.
- ⚠️ means I think the question is wrong, ambiguous or badly worded. **I recommend striking every ⚠️ one.** Three of them look like real errors in the product banks, listed in the next section.
- All of these are CivicLearn's own practice questions, not official exam questions. The page will say so.

## ⚠️ Possible errors in the live banks (worth fixing in the products anyway)

1. **Sweden SE-0829**, "Vilket år grundades FN?" The bank's answer and explanation say **1946**, but the UN was founded in **1945**. That alone explains its 43% "wrong" rate.
2. **Luxembourg LUX_00138**, ECHR year. The bank and the explanations file say **1951**. The Convention was signed on 4 Nov **1950** and came into force in 1953. Please check what the official *Vivre ensemble* material says. If it says 1951, the product should follow it, but that date shouldn't go on a world-facing page.
3. **Romania ro-000326**, "longest river flowing entirely in Romania". The bank says **Mureș**, but the Mureș flows on into Hungary. The **Olt** is the river usually given.

Also: a few of the worst Danish PR questions ("Hvem træffer afgørelse om samvær…", 62.5% wrong, and "Hvad er regeringens rolle?") only exist in the obsolete `denmark-pr/` bank, so I left them out.

## 🇩🇰 Denmark (citizenship + permanent residency): 📊 data

**DK-1012**: 56.5% wrong · 658 users
> Hvornår fik kvinder valgret til sogne- og byråd?
- **EN:** When did women get the vote in parish and town council elections?
- ✅ **1908** · 1903 · 1915 · 1921
- *Why hard:* women got the local vote seven years before the national vote (1915), and most people guess 1915.

- **EN:** When did Greenland leave the EC/EU after a referendum?

- ✅ **They besieged the city** · bombarded it from the sea · took it without a fight · withdrew immediately
- *Why hard:* people know the Swedes won big in 1658 (Roskilde) and assume the city fell.

**DK-2030**: 54.6% wrong · 603 users
> Hvornår fik Færøerne hjemmestyre ved lov?
- **EN:** When were the Faroe Islands granted home rule by law?
- ✅ **1948** · 1945 · 1953 · 1979
- *Why hard:* it gets mixed up with Greenland's home rule (1979).

**DK-2268**: 54.1% wrong · 377 users

- **EN:** Which Danish scientist won the 1997 Nobel Prize in Chemistry?
- ✅ **Jens Christian Skou** · Morten Meldal · Niels Bohr · Lene Vestergaard Hau
- *Why hard:* Meldal (2022) is the chemistry laureate people remember, and Bohr is the famous name.

**DK-1709**: 52.7% wrong · 632 users
> Hvornår blev homoseksualitet fjernet fra Sundhedsstyrelsens liste over psykiske sygdomme?
